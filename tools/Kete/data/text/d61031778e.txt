He kākano i ruia mai i Rangiātea.
Extended Whānau is an independent, Māori-owned creative consultancy exploring how indigenous knowledge can shape the future through design.
We work at the intersection of culture, strategy and storytelling. Creating design that deepens people's connection to place, to one another, and to the stories that define us.
Guided by Māori values, we collaborate with iwi, government, businesses and cultural institutions to create work that is uniquely of Aotearoa, yet universal in its appeal.
